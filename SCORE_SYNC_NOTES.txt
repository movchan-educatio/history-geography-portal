CENTRAL SCORE SYNCHRONIZATION
==============================

All game score writes now go through portal-score.js:
- History quiz: awardHistoryQuizScore
- Geography quiz: awardGeographyQuizScore
- NMT History/Geography: awardNmtScore
- World Map: awardMapPoint
- Historical Chronolog: awardHistoryActivityPoint
- Truth or Lie: awardTruthOrLiePoint

Every central score write updates:
- users/{uid}/score (lifetime total)
- the source-specific lifetime field
- the source-specific weekly field
- users/{uid}/week_score (recalculated from all weekly score components)
- lastScoreSource / lastScorePoints / lastScoreAt

If the user completes an activity before Firebase Auth is ready, the result is queued in localStorage and uploaded after sign-in.

Dino Runner keeps its best-score leaderboard in:
- dinoLeaderboard/{uid}

The Dino leaderboard is loaded only after authentication because the current Realtime Database rules require auth for reads. It updates in realtime with onValue().
